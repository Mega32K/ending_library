# Pair persistent status with transient notices

The Camera Animation Editor uses dual feedback for meaningful operation results. The Editor Status Bar remains the persistent, queryable source of truth for connection, synchronization, persistence, collaboration, permission, lock, runtime, and diagnostic state. A short-lived `Transient Status Notice` may additionally appear near the lower status region, preferably above the status bar and within the responsive available bounds, to make a newly completed, failed, cancelled, or synchronized operation discoverable without forcing the user to inspect the bar.

A Transient Status Notice is non-blocking and passive. It never takes keyboard focus, captures pointer or controller input, pauses playback, changes the playhead, changes selection, alters the viewport or runtime camera, mutates project content, or enters Undo/Redo. Selecting the notice opens the relevant read-only Status Detail Surface; it does not repeat the operation or bypass a required confirmation. Field validation, overwrite confirmation, permission confirmation, lock acquisition, and other action-required decisions remain on their dedicated property or blocking surfaces rather than being reduced to a notice.

Only one notice is visibly presented at a time. Equivalent repeated results use Status Notice Coalescing with concise aggregate context, while the pending notice set remains finite and uses deterministic oldest-first eviction. An action-required conflict or failure may not be hidden by a lower-priority success notice; the persistent Status Priority State remains authoritative and lower-priority details stay discoverable through the status surface. A notice never competes with or replaces an active Blocking Transaction Surface; the blocking surface owns its complete confirmation or progress lifecycle, and the terminal result may then be reflected in the status bar and, when useful, one notice.

Notices are client-local and connected-session-scoped. They are not project content, checkpoints, revisions, journal entries, runtime publication records, device preferences, or permanent chat history. The visible and pending notice state is released on connection, world, server, or relevant project-session exit. Every notice has truthful localized text, a semantic icon or non-color cue, an accessible dismissal path, and a route to bounded detail when additional context is available.

Transient connection loss is represented persistently by the Editor Status Bar rather than a repeating notice. One notice may report successful Reconnect Revalidation or terminal reconnection failure after the state transition, subject to the normal coalescing and lifecycle rules.
