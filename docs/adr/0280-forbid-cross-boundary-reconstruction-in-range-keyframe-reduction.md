# Forbid cross-boundary reconstruction in range keyframe reduction

`Tracks in Time Range` applies a Key-Reduction Boundary Guard to its closed Time Scope. A provisional deletion is eligible only when every replacement `LINEAR` segment lies entirely inside that interval. If either surviving neighbor lies outside such that their replacement connection would cross the start or end, the candidate is Boundary Protected, including a candidate exactly on a range endpoint. This protection remains even when the replacement would be mathematically collinear, evaluate with zero measured error, or otherwise pass the selected Trajectory Fidelity Profile.

The operation never inserts a Bake Boundary Anchor or another compensating keyframe because Remove Redundant Keyframes is a deletion tool. Every out-of-range node retains its identity, time, value, and interpolation adjacency, and every out-of-range segment retains its original structure. Trajectory Fidelity Profile tolerance is used only for permitted reconstruction inside the range and cannot authorize an outside structural change.

The Tool Operation Preview identifies Boundary Protected candidates separately from interpolation- or fidelity-protected nodes and suggests expanding the range, choosing Full Animation, or deliberately switching to Selected Keyframes. Selected Keyframes is an identity scope rather than a temporal cut, so it may evaluate the complete interval between unselected fixed anchors under the normal fidelity rules; it does not inherit the range guard unless a range mode is explicitly chosen.
