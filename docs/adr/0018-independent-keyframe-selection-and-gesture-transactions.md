# Keep keyframes independent while supporting professional multi-selection

Every keyframe has a stable identity within its scalar track. Equal timestamps do not merge keyframes across tracks, and moving or editing one keyframe does not implicitly bind it to another. The editor supports marquee selection, track selection, and modifier-key multi-selection through a temporary Selection Set. A plain left click replaces the selection, `Ctrl` plus left click adds or toggles a node, and `Shift` plus left click removes a node. `Shift` is not a range-selection shortcut. Dragging an empty timeline region creates a marquee selection, `Ctrl` adds or toggles the marquee result, `Shift` removes it, and clicking empty space clears the selection.

A linked or batch gesture may operate on many selected keys and may lock time, lock value, or coordinate related channels such as translation axes. The client captures a Gesture Baseline and expected revision when the gesture begins, then keeps pointer movement as local predicted state plus a throttled ephemeral Gesture Preview. The server does not receive a durable content operation for every drag frame. On release or explicit numeric confirmation, the client submits the complete atomic Edit Gesture Transaction against that baseline; acceptance creates one authoritative revision, one Operation Journal entry, and one Personal Edit History item even when the transaction contains many field-level changes. A drag may explicitly overwrite same-track, same-lane targets according to the Drag Overwrite rule, and those removals remain inside the same transaction. Cancellation, `Esc`, focus loss with cancellation semantics, or connection loss discards the uncommitted preview without changing project content.

Under ADR-0206, a Transient Connection Interruption cancels only the active Gesture Preview to its pre-gesture baseline. It does not clear unrelated Retained Reconnect Drafts or Project Workspace State.

Tools Menu retiming, baking, and optimization operations use the same atomic boundary after their Tool Operation Preview is confirmed; their generated multi-track and structural changes are never split into per-key or per-track history entries.
